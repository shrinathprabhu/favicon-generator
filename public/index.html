Favicon Generator

A simple sharp wrapper that generates favicon for your apps with a single SVG

What It Generates

Each download includes favicon.ico, favicon.svg, PNG sizes from 16x16 to 256x256, Apple touch icon, Android Chrome icons, Windows tile icon, site.webmanifest, browserconfig.xml, html-tags.txt, and the original source image.
